#4471 was merged as one issue: the parser reads dates month first. That is true for most of its reports. It is not true for all of them. Counted by cause, the reports fall into three problems, and each has its own ticket in grzegorzp-commits/kb-4471.

Not known and fixed. The parser fix will not close the form hint reports or the one-day moves.

The three problems

  1. Parser (ticket 1). The shared parser reads every date month first, so day-first tenants get flipped dates. This is the cause the incident page names, and it covers most of the reports.
  2. Hint (ticket 2). Tenants on booking-v3 see the hint MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type month first, and the form reads it day first. Every one of these reports came through the form. Intake tagged many of them parser, because the tag is a guess from the browser.
  3. One-day move (ticket 3). Five reports describe a date that is one day off, not a swapped month. The desk's own notes list them. The table above files them under parser because of their tenant; they look like a time zone problem.

Why the merge hid this

The desk merges on subject line (decision 0001). Subject says nothing about cause, so three different bugs went into one ticket and were counted as one. The intake tag does not help: it is a guess made before anyone reads the report.

Lessons

  • Count by cause, not by subject. The cause depends on the tenant and the form, not on the words.
  • A ticket marked known can still hide other problems. Check the table before you close it.
